Company profile

F4 Drones

Brief: F4 Drones is a drone-parts and build-support brand concept for pilots and makers who want carefully described components, practical guidance, and the confidence to create a setup that suits their flying.

Our fuller story

We bring the essentials of an FPV drone build into one approachable place: propulsion, frames, flight electronics, power, wiring, and FPV video parts. Whether you are replacing a worn propeller or assembling a custom freestyle quad, our aim is to make the options easier to compare and the next step easier to understand.

F4 Drones puts clear specifications and responsible setup advice first. We encourage builders to check frame dimensions, connector types, voltage ranges, firmware support, and local regulations before ordering or flying. A component that fits your mission—and the rest of your build—is more useful than a part chosen by headline specs alone.

What we aim to offer

  • A focused range of parts for custom FPV quadcopter builds.
  • Useful product details to help with fit and compatibility decisions.
  • Friendly guidance for assembly, maintenance, and upgrades.
  • A community-minded approach to safe, responsible flying.
